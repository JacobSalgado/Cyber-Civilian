**Platform:**
Unity Version 6.2

**Genre:**
Bullet-Hell/Dungeon Crawler

**Synoposis:**
_Cyber Civilian_ stars the Criminal who is imprisoned in the Prison Space Ark for his past crimes.  The Ark has been overtaken by the Cyberboss and his gang of Cybergoons.  Because of his impressive criminal history, the Criminal is given a chance by the Ark authorities for redemption; take out the Cyberboss and his crew and his criminal history is wiped away,  Armed with his Correctional Ship, the Criminal takes off to clear the hordes of prisoners and to take down the Cyberboss.
